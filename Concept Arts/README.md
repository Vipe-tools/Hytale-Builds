# Concept Arts

This folder contains concept builds that are intended to serve as templates and starting points for future Hytale builds.

These builds focus primarily on the basic structure, layout, proportions, and overall shape rather than finished textures, decoration, or visual details.

They can be used as a foundation for multiple different designs. For example, a waiting room or lobby concept may keep the same basic layout while its materials, textures, decorations, and overall style are completely changed.

Concept builds are therefore not intended to represent finished buildings. They are rough structural ideas that can be developed into more detailed builds later.
